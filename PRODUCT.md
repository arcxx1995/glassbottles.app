# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Curious, playful people drawn to randomness and serendipity. They open the app in a light mood — "what will I get today?" — not to scroll, not to connect with known people, but to experience the surprise of an anonymous exchange. Context: a commute, a coffee break, a quiet ten minutes. One deliberate moment, not a session.

## Product Purpose

glassbottles is an anonymous daily message exchange. Each user gets one throw per day — write a message, send it to a random stranger anywhere, and wait to see what washes up in your own inbox. The randomness is the product. Success looks like a user returning tomorrow because yesterday's bottle left them curious, amused, or unexpectedly moved.

## Brand Personality

Playful · Surprising · Warm

Voice: light and unhurried, with a hint of wonder. Not precious, not edgy. The tone of finding a lucky coin on the pavement, not the tone of a love letter or a startup pitch. Copy stays short and human — never overwrought, never clinical.

## Anti-references

- **Dark moody SaaS** (Notion, Linear): serious, productivity-coded, cold precision. glassbottles is joyful, not efficient.
- **Social media feed** (Twitter, Instagram): follower counts, likes, algorithmic feeds, engagement metrics. glassbottles has no feed, no metrics, no public profiles.
- **Dating app** (Tinder, Bumble): swipe mechanics, profile photos, match-making energy. The anonymity must be total and the stakes low.
- **Literal 'bottle' nostalgia**: pirate maps, wax seals, parchment, aged paper. The ocean metaphor earns its place through color and motion, not scrapbook texture.

## Design Principles

1. **Serendipity over control.** The not-knowing is the point. Don't add preferences, filters, or recipient selection. The randomness must feel genuinely random.
2. **The ritual is the retention.** One throw per day makes each message feel significant. Protect the anticipation — don't shortcut it with skips, reminders, or urgency mechanics.
3. **Warmth over polish.** Human and approachable beats premium-cold. Imperfect texture and light copy beat surgical precision.
4. **Show, don't scroll.** This is not a feed. Avoid infinite scroll, metric counts, engagement loops. Each moment in the app is complete in itself.
5. **Surprise as delight, not stress.** The randomness should feel exciting. Tone stays warm and easy even when nothing has arrived yet.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AAA where achievable: enhanced contrast ratios (7:1 for body text where feasible), keyboard navigability throughout, screen reader semantics, explicit reduced-motion alternatives for all animations (glassbottles is motion-native; `prefers-reduced-motion` must be a first-class path, not an afterthought). No time-gated interactions that create anxiety for users with cognitive differences.
