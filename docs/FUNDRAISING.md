---

## Is this an MVP?

Not yet. What exists today = a **prototype / demo**: one clever mechanic (bottle throw),
no retention loop, no monetization, no proof anyone comes back. That's pre-MVP.

MVP for the thesis = the smallest thing that proves the **retention loop works**:
bottle throw + mood check-in + streak + "bottle was found" push. That's the spine.
Until people *come back daily on their own*, there is no MVP — only a toy that demos well.

The good news: that MVP is small. It's all in the existing stack (Supabase RPC + Realtime
+ PWA). Weeks, not months. It can be built without funding.

---

## Can you raise right now?

Honestly — **hard, and probably not the smart move yet.** The blunt reasoning:

- Consumer social is the **graveyard category** for VCs. The bar is brutal. They've been
  burned by "wholesome social app" pitches a hundred times.
- Current position: an idea, a positioning, a prototype, a solo (?) founder, **zero
  retention data, zero users, zero revenue.** That's an idea-stage raise. Idea-stage
  consumer-social raises happen almost exclusively for **founders with a track record**
  (prior exit, ex-FAANG, famous in the space). If that's not you, a cold pre-seed on this
  alone is a long shot.
- The thing investors fund in consumer is **traction, not vision.** The vision is good —
  but vision is cheap, retention curves are not.

So: you *can* try to raise now, but you'd be raising on the weakest possible position and
likely burn your best intros getting nos.

---

## What actually gets you funded

**Build the MVP first, get a retention signal, THEN raise.** Sequence:

1. **Ship the retention MVP** (mood + streak + bottle-found push). ~2–6 weeks, self-funded
   with $0 — the stack is already cheap (Supabase-direct, near-zero marginal cost; that low
   burn IS part of the pitch).
2. **Get 200–1,000 real users.** Launch where lonely/ND people already gather: ADHD TikTok,
   r/ADHD, r/lonely, neurodivergent Twitter/Discord, Product Hunt. The bottle moment is
   screenshot-able — that's free distribution. Cost: your time.
3. **Measure D1/D7/D30 retention by cohort.** This single chart is the whole game. If D30
   holds even ~15–25% on a wholesome social app, you have a *real* fundraising story. If it
   doesn't, no amount of money fixes it — and you've saved yourself raising on a dud.
4. **Then raise pre-seed** ($150k–500k) on: "here's a working loop, here's a retention curve
   that bends right, here's organic pull from a desperate underserved audience, here's a
   near-zero cost structure, here's the B2B wellness scale story." *That* is fundable.

## How to fund the gap (you can't self-fund)

You don't need VC to build the MVP — you need *almost nothing* because the infra is cheap.
But to cover yourself + small costs:

- **Grants / non-dilutive first.** This thesis (loneliness, mental health, neurodivergence)
  is grant-magnetic. Mental-health foundations, social-impact funds, neurodiversity orgs,
  university innovation grants. Free money, no equity, and a grant on your CV de-risks you
  for later VCs.
- **Accelerators** — apply to **YC, On Deck, EF (Entrepreneur First), South Park Commons.**
  EF and SPC specifically back people *pre-idea/pre-traction* — exactly your stage if you
  lack a track record. YC funds prototypes with a strong founder story + early signal.
  Accelerator = money + stamp + intros, the fastest path for a first-time founder with no
  network.
- **Angels over VCs at this stage.** Mission-aligned angels (people who've lived
  loneliness/ADHD, mental-health operators) write small checks on conviction + early
  traction. Warmer, faster, less proof needed than a fund.
- **Crowdfunding fits the story** — a lonely/community app raising *from its own community*
  (Wefunder/Republic) is on-narrative and doubles as marketing. Later, once you have users.
